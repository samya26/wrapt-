How Wrapt works
  1. 1 · The design plan

    The AI never paints a picture. It writes a small JSON "design plan" — name, colours, fonts, layout, pattern, border, logo shape and the reasons behind each choice. Wrapt's own engine draws it, so every result stays editable, fast and cheap. Without AI, a built-in rule-based designer writes the plan.

  2. 2 · Three concepts

    One brief becomes three different directions (e.g. Premium, Minimal, Traditional). Each is rendered in 3D off-screen and shown as a thumbnail so you can compare.

  3. 3 · Logo Studio

    No logo? Draw your idea or describe it. Your sketch is stored as point lists, simplified (Ramer–Douglas–Peucker), wobbly circles and lines are snapped to perfect shapes, and the rest is smoothed into Bézier curves. The AI then writes six logo "recipes" (layout, font, spacing, detail, symbol) — never pictures — and Wrapt draws them as crisp vectors. Quality rules are enforced in code: typography first, symbols must fit the product, no text collisions, every logo tested small and in one colour.

  4. 4 · Reading colours

    Uploaded logos and reference photos are shrunk to 64×64 pixels and a tiny k-means clustering finds their main colours.

  5. 5 · Patterns & borders

    Indian art patterns are drawn as small repeating tiles. All 16 borders are drawn with code, so they fit any panel size. Uploaded borders use 9-slice scaling: the image is cut into a 3×3 grid; corners are copied as-is and only the edges stretch, so one border fits every panel.

  6. 6 · UV mapping

    Every 3D vertex has a (u, v) coordinate saying which pixel of the flat label sits there — that's how a rectangle wraps a can or a dieline lands on six box faces.

  7. 7 · The fold

    The box is a tree of hinged panels; animating the hinge angles folds the flat dieline into a box.

  8. 8 · Design check

    An automatic audit measures text contrast (WCAG ratios), whether the logo blends into the pack, logo size on curved surfaces, edge distance and pattern strength — each with a one-click fix.

  9. 9 · Realism & export

    Physically based materials, environment lighting, soft shadows and filmic tone mapping. Images are re-rendered at exact Instagram sizes; the 360° video is recorded from the WebGL canvas with MediaRecorder.

Built with Three.js and plain JavaScript; the AI runs through a small serverless function (Gemini). Your files never leave your browser except the brief and reference photos you send to the AI.
